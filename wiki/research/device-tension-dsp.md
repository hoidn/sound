# Tension audio by extracted execution (T1.2)

**Question.** What does the bundled Tension instrument output for a stated parameter set and stated
note events? T1.2 of [roadmap revision 3](native-decompilation-roadmap.md#revision-3--t1-completion-plan-2026-10-18)
needs this for every one of the seven bundled devices. Which measurements describe that output, and
which comparison metric suits each kind of render? The method follows the Corpus gate in
[device DSP by extracted execution](device-dsp-extracted.md).

**Answer (X, draft).**

- **Rendering.** The Tension x86_64 slice renders notes through its own `CalcA` under the bundle loader. All 68 retained renders and
  about 2,000 check renders completed without a trap or fault.
- **Determinism.** With constant parameters a render is bit-identical across:
  - re-runs;
  - host blocks of 64 and 256 frames against 512;
  - flush-to-zero with denormals-are-zero;
  - three clock seeds.

  Two settings are the exceptions: KeyboardError above 0, and VibratoError above 0 with vibrato on. Their output depends on the
  clock seed.
- **Events.** A note takes effect on the 1 ms control grid that [Analog](device-dsp-extracted.md#analog-results) uses, by the
  same rule. Note-on and note-off sweeps at both rates and three block sizes confirm it.
- **The string.** After the excitation the string rings as a set of slightly inharmonic partials. Their frequencies, decay
  rates and complex amplitudes are measured with the [Corpus estimator](device-dsp-extracted.md#static-model), applied to each
  partial.
- **Floating-point sensitivity.** For plucked, struck, damped, filtered and body cases it is small. One-ulp libm perturbations
  and directed rounding of every SSE operation move the waveform by at most 6 × 10⁻⁴ (relative RMS) while the note is held
  and 2.3 × 10⁻³ after the note-off. Partial frequencies move by at most 4.7 × 10⁻⁷ (relative).
- **Chaotic settings.** The bow excitator, and the damper at mass 0, respond chaotically to the same perturbations. The
  bouncing hammer sits in between.
- **Metric.** These results set the metric of [SPEC-DEVICE-TENSION](../specs/device-tension.md#comparison-metric).

Evidence: [t12-tension-v1](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/r2/t12-tension-v1/manifest.json), regenerated by `sh run.sh`
(about two minutes, 13.7 MB). Its parts:

- `harness/`: the loader, copied from t12-device-dsp-v1 with one changed file;
- `tension.py`: host values and scripts;
- `cases.py`: the 68 cases;
- `raw/<case>.wav` and `raw/<case>.script`: the retained renders and their exact calls;
- `results/runs.json`: hashes, imports and per-block flags;
- `results/measure.json` and `results/analyze.txt`: measurements of every render;
- `results/checks.json`: timing, determinism, quantization, pitch, level, polyphony and filter linearity;
- `results/sensitivity.json`: the perturbation study;
- `results/fma_scan.txt`.

## Slice and harness

Observed (`manifest.json` `external_inputs`). The executable is
`Contents/App-Resources/Devices/Mac/Tension.bundle/Contents/MacOS/Tension` (SHA-256 `db7eb728…077c6f`). Its x86_64 slice
is at offset 16,384 and is 501,392 bytes long, with SHA-256 `63dd07171e76e20427045cc0269e124d67e94e6cddb42cab82c77cec7e6ed05d`
(`MH_BUNDLE`). Both values equal the t12 probe's. The arm64 slice is at offset 524,288 and is 449,696 bytes long, with SHA-256
`640700be…b424be`.

The loader is the [t12 harness](device-dsp-extracted.md#harness), built to `/var/tmp/sound-analysis/t12-tension/xdev`. Only
`harness/xdev_bind.c` differs from the t12 copy, and an unperturbed render is unchanged by the difference:

- **Exact results.** Under `XDEV_LIBM_ULP`, integer-valued libm results such as `expf(0) = 1` are no longer moved. Any libm
  that returns exact cases exactly gives those values.
- **Function selection.** `XDEV_LIBM_ONLY` restricts the perturbation to named functions.

Cross-check: this loader reproduces the retained Corpus render `pitch_0.25` bit for bit. Its perturbed Corpus results equal
t12's (relative RMS 3.94 × 10⁻² and 3.98 × 10⁻² over the window), so the t12 Corpus figures do not depend on the
exact-case change.

**Imports.** Every Tension import the renders call is in t12's binding table. The render calls `rand` 129 times, seeded
once by `srand(time(0))` while the engine is built. It also calls `expf` 1,095 times, `logf` 5,661 times and `tanf` 416
times during construction, before any note. Per note it calls `exp` (`results/runs.json` `imports`).

## How the renders are driven

A render follows the call order and conversions of
[device parameter mapping](device-parameter-mapping-native.md#how-live-loads-a-bundle) and
[device tables](device-tables-native.md#tension-stringstudio-130-entries-0129-25-second-outputs-1-unused):

1. `CreateInstanceU`;
2. `SetSampleRateU` (44,100 or 48,000);
3. `SetParameterA` for the 129 indices 0 to 129 except 2, in index order, with the values Live sends;
4. `CalcA` in 512-frame blocks, with silent buffers, input flag 0 and transport {0, 0.0, 120.0};
5. 16-byte note events {frame, status, d1, d2}, with the frame relative to the block that contains it.

`tension.py` derives host values from stored values with N6's per-row mode. It checks itself against N6's
`sent_for_default`: 101 values are stated there and all agree. The others are second outputs and toggle entries, derived as
N6 states.

Each case of `cases.py` states:

- its stored-value changes, applied to the constructor defaults or to a shipped preset;
- its note: C4 (note 60) at velocity 100 unless stated, on at frame 4,096 and off 22,050 frames later (24,000 at 48 kHz);
- one second of output.

Every render's two channels are bit-identical, so each is retained as mono IEEE-float WAV.

## Determinism, protocol and timing

`results/checks.json` `determinism`:

| Variant against `defaults_44` | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Re-run; blocks of 64 and 256; MXCSR `0x9fc0`; clock 1,760,000,000 and 12,345 | Bit-identical |
| Blocks of 333 | Differs from frame 4,125: the note lands on another grid point (below) |
| Reset sequence (1,024 frames of processing, `ResetA`, index 0 again), with or without the index-0 resend | Bit-identical |
| Rate 44,100 then 48,000 against 48,000 directly | Max 1.8 × 10⁻⁷, rel RMS 4.4 × 10⁻⁷ |
| KeyboardError 1; Vibrato on with VibratoError 0.5 | Seed-dependent (max 0.19; 8.6 × 10⁻⁵) |
| Vibrato on with VibratoError 0; LFO shapes 0 to 4 on the filter; Unison on | Seed-independent |
| No events; ExcitatorToggle off; ExcitatorVelocityVelMod 1 at velocity 127 | Output exactly zero throughout |
| Body on with BodyMix 0 | Bit-identical to Body off |

**Event timing** (`timing`). Analog's rule holds:

1. The device keeps a grid of P = ⌊fs/1000⌋ frames (44 or 48) counted from the first processed frame.
2. An event at frame f takes effect at the start g of the period that contains f.
3. If g lies in an earlier host block, it takes effect one period later instead.

Tested on 140 note-ons each at 44.1 kHz (blocks 512, 64, 333) and at 48 kHz, and on 100 note-offs in each of five
settings. An event renders bit-identically to the same event placed at its effective frame.

From the effective frame, the first non-zero output frame follows after a fixed delay, which depends on the excitator
type and slightly on velocity. The first frame that differs from the held note follows a note-off after a fixed delay
that depends on the setting:

| Setting | Note-on delay (frames, velocity 1 / 64 / 127) | Note-off delay (frames) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type 0 (bow) | 13 / 13 / 14 | 14 |
| Types 1 and 2 (hammer, bouncing hammer) | 17 / 17 / 18 | 78 (type 1) |
| Type 3 (plectrum, default) | 32 / 32 / 33 (36 at 48 kHz) | 78 (85 at 48 kHz) |
| Plectrum, damper on and gated | — | 66 |

A note-on with velocity 0 acts as a note-off (bit-identical).

## Controller quantization

The Tension bundle declares most indices as kind 0 ([device bundles](device-bundles-static.md#where-the-parameter-model-lives)).
On these indices the device turns a host value v into the controller c = 128v for v ≤ 0.5 and 126v + 1 above 0.5. This is
the rule SPEC-DEVICE-CORPUS REQ-003 gives for Corpus.

`quantization` was tested with every section switched on, on note 72 at velocity 90:

- **Stepped, 29 indices.** The excitator, damper, string, termination and body controls use ⌊c⌋. ExcitatorVelocity's
  steps start exactly at 1/128 and at 0.5, and above 0.5 within one binary32 ulp of (k − 1)/126.
- **Continuous, 12 indices.** KeyboardFineTune, KeyboardStretch, KeyboardError, VibratoDelay, PortamentoTime, the filter
  cutoff and resonance with their key and envelope amounts, and BodyLevel.
- **No change at 0.25 against 0.253, 26 indices.** The modulation sources (vibrato, LFO, filter envelope) are among them;
  their effects need time or an amount that this context did not give. So are the three positions, which are coarser.

**Positions** take a set of discrete values that depends on the note. Over the 129 host values k/128, with the damper and
pickup on:

| Parameter | Distinct renders at note 60 | at note 36 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GeoExcitatorPosition | 34 | 95 |
| GeoDamperPosition | 36 | 87 |
| PickupPosition | 35 | 98 |

The steps narrow toward 1. Inferred: the string is sampled at points whose number grows with the string's period in frames.

estimator on 40,000-frame renders.

| Measured Hz | 32.702 | 65.405 | 130.810 | 261.620 | 523.237 | 1046.432 | 2092.226 |

- **Fractional shifts are slightly sharp.** Transpose +7 gives +1.11 cents, and pitch bend at range 2 gives +1.13, +0.71,

  the range in semitones.
- **KeyboardFineTune** gives −49.58, −24.59, +25.20 and +50.00 cents (relative to its default) at 0, 0.25, 0.75 and 1.
- **KeyboardStretch** detunes in proportion to the distance from note 69: about 0.8 cents per semitone at Stretch 0, toward
  sharp below A4, half that at 0.25, and the opposite sign at 1. C2 is +26.40, +13.10 and −26.17 cents; C4 is +7.20, +3.57

## String partials

Measured over the held window with `measure.modes`: partial k is predicted from the first partial and a running
inharmonicity fit f_k = k·f₁·√((1 + Bk²)/(1 + B)), then measured. The measurements are frequency, T60 = −3 ln 10/(ρ·fs) with
ρ the per-frame log radius, and level relative to the strongest partial.

**Defaults, C4, 44.1 kHz** (`defaults_44`):

| k | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hz | 261.620 | 524.522 | 789.533 | 1056.900 | 1326.471 | 1597.919 | 1870.893 | 2134.901 |
| T60, s | 5.552 | 3.642 | 2.301 | 1.510 | 1.044 | 0.757 | 0.571 | 0.448 |
| Level, dB | 0 | −2.6 | −8.6 | −22.3 | −18.6 | −15.0 | −16.5 | −22.2 |

- **48 kHz.** The partials differ slightly: 524.251, 788.590 and 1054.908 Hz for k = 2 to 4, with B 7.6 × 10⁻⁴ against
  8.4 × 10⁻⁴. A render at 48 kHz is therefore not a resampled 44.1 kHz render.
- **By note.** At C2 the T60s run from 5.81 s (k = 1) to 4.49 s (k = 8), with B 3.8 × 10⁻⁴. At C5 they are 2.83, 1.00 and
  0.48 s, with B 1.9 × 10⁻³. At C6 only the first partial is resolved: 0.62 s.
- **StringDecay.** It sets the overall decay. The first partial lasts 0.216 s at 0.25, 1.194 s at 0.5, 5.552 s at 0.75 and
  17.56 s at 1.
- **StringDamping.** It sets how fast the upper partials decay relative to the first. At 1, k = 1 to 8 all last 6.7 to
  6.3 s. At 0 only a 258.5 Hz component of 0.05 s remains; the held RMS is 3 × 10⁻⁴.
- **StringInharmonicity.** At 0, B is 5 × 10⁻⁶ (harmonic: k = 2 is at 523.244 Hz). At 1, B is 3.7 × 10⁻³ (k = 2 is at
  530.355 Hz). The first partial stays at 261.620 Hz in both.
- **StringDecayRatio** sets the decay after the note-off. The release T60s of the first partial are:

  | Ratio | 0 | 0.25 | 0.5 (default) | 0.75 | 1 |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Release T60 of k = 1, s | 32.98 | 1.134 | 0.205 | 0.091 | Stops within 50 ms |

  At ratio 1 the last non-zero frame is 30,562.
- **Key amounts** act relative to C4: StringDecayKbdMod ±1 and StringDampingKbdMod 1 have no effect at note 60. At notes 36
  and 84 they do.
- **Voice end.** After the release the output becomes exactly zero. In `defaults_44` the last non-zero frame is 42,591, and
  the largest magnitude in the 10 ms before it is 6.0 × 10⁻⁷. The rule that ends the voice was not determined.

## Filter

Off in the constructor defaults; on in the Defaults preset at type 1 with cutoff 1. Live sends FilterType t as ⌊t/2⌋ on
index 91 and t mod 2 on index 92.

**Method.** The response is measured on a bright C2 string (`filt_base`: hammer, Stiffness 1, StringDamping 1, StringDecay 1,
StringInharmonicity 0). It is the complex ratio of the filtered render to `filt_base` at the 318 harmonic peaks from 65 Hz to
20.9 kHz, over the held window.

**Linearity** (`filter_linearity`). Lowering the excitation by 8 and 22 dB changes the measured response by at most
0.092 dB and 0.0065 rad (type 1 at Q 0 and 0.5), and by 0.0004 dB for type 8. The string's decays are unchanged through
the filter.

## Output level and polyphony

**BodyLevel** (the Volume control) scales the output exactly. Let n = c/127, with c = 128v or 126v + 1 unrounded, and n = 0.5
at c = 64. Then the gain relative to the default 0.705 is (n/n(0.705))². Ten values from 0 to 1 agree within 2.6 × 10⁻⁷
(`level`). At 1 the gain is 1.99878 (+6.0 dB); at 0 the output is exactly zero.

**Polyphony** (`polyphony`). The first note is C1. Later notes, each a different pitch, start 441 frames apart, and the
first note is tested for silence:

| Stored Polyphony | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 (default) | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Voices | 1 | 2 | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 | 24 | 28 | 32 |

The note one beyond the voice count silences the oldest voice. In mono, the stolen voice's output changes at the new note's
effective frame. Repeating one pitch does not use up voices: C1 survives eight repeats of C5 with four voices.

## Floating-point sensitivity and the metric

`sensitivity.py` re-renders 31 cases five ways:

- with every inexact libm result one ulp up;
- one ulp down;
- with MXCSR rounding toward −∞, +∞ and 0 for every SSE operation in the bundle.

Directed rounding stands in for per-operation rounding differences of the arm64 slice. That slice has 822 fused
multiply-adds in 109 functions; 139 of them are in `0x33c9c`. The x86_64 slice has none (`results/fma_scan.txt`). Directed
rounding errs in one direction at every operation, so it is taken as an upper bound (inferred).

The table gives the worst results over the five perturbations (`results/sensitivity.json` `_summary`):

| Group | Held rel RMS | Release rel RMS | Partial frequency (rel) | Log radius (rel) | Complex amplitude (re strongest) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 26 stable cases (plectrum, hammer, damper, termination, pickup, Body, filter, presets) | 6.0 × 10⁻⁴ | 2.3 × 10⁻³ | 4.7 × 10⁻⁷ | 1.4 × 10⁻⁴ | 1.1 × 10⁻⁴ |
| Bouncing hammer | 1.1 × 10⁻³ | 1.2 × 10⁻³ | 7.3 × 10⁻⁵ | 1.4 × 10⁻² | 8.7 × 10⁻³ |
| Bow (3 cases), damper mass 0 | 1.3 | 1.5 | 9 × 10⁻² | — | — |

In the chaotic group the held RMS level moved by at most 1.0 dB, and the onset frame did not move. Third-octave levels
moved by up to 16 dB in bands within 40 dB of the strongest (Cello).

**Exact libm results matter.** With t12's perturbation, which also moves exact results, `expf` alone made the default
plectrum render differ by rel RMS 1.5. The +1 and −1 renders agreed with each other to 3 × 10⁻⁷. The flip comes from
`expf(0)` returning 1.0 + 1 ulp instead of exactly 1.0. Every libm returns `expf(0)` = 1 exactly, so this is not an Apple
against glibc risk, and the revised perturbation leaves it exact.

Consequences, stated in the spec:

- **Waveform comparison is usable** for the stable group, unlike Corpus's long modal rings. The proposed tolerances are
  3 × 10⁻³ held and 10⁻² after the note-off.
- **Partial measurements are a second comparator.** Frequency within 2 × 10⁻⁶, log radius within 6 × 10⁻⁴ and complex
  amplitude within 5 × 10⁻⁴ give margins of 4 to 5 over the worst stable case.
- **The bouncing hammer** gets a waveform tolerance of 5 × 10⁻³ over the whole render.
- **Chaotic cases** are graded only as non-silent and bounded, with the held RMS within 3 dB and the onset frame exact.

## Limits

- **What X shows.** It shows what the x86_64 Tension code computes for the stated calls. Live's side is static (N5, N6),
  with the unknowns of [Q-025](../questions.md#q-025-device-host-protocol-and-device-audio-acceptance): event frame base,
  transport and flag values, Live's block partition, and when the reset sequence runs.
- **Not covered by a description.** Vibrato, LFO (and its sync), the filter envelope, portamento, unison, pitch bend beyond
  the static bend values, channel pressure and slide (MPE), the mod wheel, the sustain pedal, and mono-mode priority and
  legato. Except LFO sync and the random settings, these are deterministic, so on-demand renders can grade them; no signal
  description exists.
- **Substitutions.** glibc libm, the inferred Park–Miller `rand` (used only by the seed-dependent settings), the fixed clock
  and the libc++ stream shim. No stream extraction failed.
- **arm64** was not executed; its fused operations are only counted.
- **Measurements are descriptive.** The estimator resolves partials at least 60 dB above the floor; partials of high notes
  decay too fast to resolve beyond the first. The filter and Body tables are measured at harmonic frequencies of one string.
