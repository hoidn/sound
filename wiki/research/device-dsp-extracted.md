# Device audio by extracted execution (T1.2 feasibility gate)

**Question.** Workstream T1.2 of [roadmap revision 3](native-decompilation-roadmap.md#revision-3--t1-completion-plan-2026-10-18)
starts with a gate. Can a harness load a device bundle's x86_64 slice, drive it the way Live does
and render audio? Are the renders deterministic and bit-for-bit repeatable? Can they be explained
by the static [Corpus DSP model](corpus-dsp-static.md)? Can the instrument path (note events in,
audio out) be driven? What metric should a device spec state, and what would the other five
devices need?

**Current reading boundary (2026-10-20).** The feasibility results below describe
the historical t12 packet. Current Corpus acceptance criteria and ungraded modal
omissions belong to the [canonical draft](../specs/device-corpus.md) and
[current released handoff](../../handoff/device-corpus-v5/README.md). Aggregate
historical modal comparisons and perturbation results below are not general
frequency/error bounds, full-profile coverage or Live/arm64 compatibility proof.
The [build-provenance gate](corpus-dsp-static.md#build-provenance-gate--2026-10-20)
records prerequisites for that parked observer/setup route. It does not gate
unchanged standing harness batches. Current selections follow
[plan revision4, second amendment](full-live-specification-plan.md#second-amendment--batch-adoption-authorization-scope-and-x-count-ordering-2026-10-21)
and [design12](full-live-specification-design.md#revision-12--review-once-batch-adoption-and-authorization-scope--2026-10-21):
one frozen standing packet, one sole independent actual/source review. D-01 is
answered; public/ARM/receiver and unsupported required behavior remain U.

**Historical answer (X, draft). The finite feasibility gate passed.**

- **Loading.** A loader adapted from the [extracted-execution harness](extracted-execution.md)
  loads the Corpus, Analog, Tension, Collision and Electric slices. It creates instances and
  renders through the bundles' own `CalcA`. No exception path, fault or unbound import was hit.
- **Repeatability.** Corpus renders are bit-identical in these cases:
  - re-runs in new processes (6 of 6);
  - host blocks of 64, 256, 333 and 2,048 frames against 512, with constant parameters;
  - flush-to-zero and denormals-are-zero against the default MXCSR;
  - another clock value for the random seed, except where a random LFO shape is selected.
- **Static model.** Against the [static model](corpus-dsp-static.md) of the modal resonator:
  - mode frequencies agree within about one binary32 ulp of the mode's filter coefficients
    (41 impulse cases, both channels);
  - decay rates agree within about two ulps;
  - mode amplitudes, and with them the strike and pickup position effects, agree to 2.8 × 10⁻⁴
    (Membrane, with closely spaced modes, 2.3 × 10⁻³);
  - steady-state sine gains agree to 2.2 × 10⁻⁴ dB, and noise third-octave levels to 0.004 dB.
- **Port.** Every difference from the [traced port](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/demos/corpus-traced/index.html)
  is explained ([differences](#differences-and-their-explanations)).
- **Instrument path.** Analog plays notes from 16-byte events. Its note-on and note-off take effect
  on a 1 ms control grid, by an exact rule that holds for all 300 tested events.
- **Other devices.** Tension and Electric render notes after six more bindings. Collision runs, but
  its parameter path is not understood yet. Amp and Cabinet stop in a static initializer at
  `dladdr` and need a much larger macOS emulation ([effort](#effort-for-the-other-five-devices)).

Evidence: [t12-device-dsp-v1](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/r2/t12-device-dsp-v1/manifest.json),
regenerated by `sh run.sh` (about one minute). The evidence directory holds:

- `harness/`: the loader sources;
- `inputs/`: the input signals;
- `raw/`: every retained render (`*.f32`, interleaved stereo binary32), its script and its log;
- `results/`: JSON results, among them `corpus_runs.json`, `corpus_model.json`, `corpus_port.json`,
  `analog.json`, `libm_sensitivity.json`, `other_devices.json` and `fma_scan.txt`;
- the scripts.

The draft behavioral contract built on this page is [SPEC-DEVICE-CORPUS](../specs/device-corpus.md).

## How Live drives a bundle

Live side (S, from [N5](device-parameter-mapping-native.md#how-live-loads-a-bundle)). Live
loads `<resources>/Devices/Mac/<Name>.bundle` and resolves the seven exports by name. It queries
`GetPlugInfoU`. It calls `CreateInstanceU()` and then `SetSampleRateU(handle, rate)` with the
current rate. Parameters are flushed as `SetParameterA(handle, index, value)` with the index
order and conversions of N5 and [N6](device-tables-native.md). The reviewed
[processing-protocol trace](device-host-protocol.md#initialization-reset-sample-rate-and-teardown)
distinguishes ordinary Reset/NewRate (no warm-up) from ResetAndDispatch: four
1,024-frame helper requests, then reset and cached index-0 restoration for the
reset-capable primary. This corrects the earlier one-warm-up shorthand; the
exact initialization/reset audio remains unknown. Live sets flush-to-zero around `SetSampleRateU`,
`SetParameterA` and `ResetA` (arm64).

Bundle side (X and S, x86_64 disassembly in this packet). The calling convention is System V.

| Export | Corpus x86_64 | Analog x86_64 | Arguments and result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CreateInstanceU` | `0x23b0` | `0x1fd70` | () → instance |
| `DestroyInstanceU` | `0x2460` | `0x1fdf0` | (instance) |
| `SetSampleRateU` | `0x2490` | `0x1fe60` | (instance, rate in Hz as a 64-bit integer) → 1 |
| `GetPlugInfoU` | `0x2930` | `0x1fed0` | (selector, out, size) → 1 if answered |
| `CalcA` | `0x29c0` | `0x1ff80` | (instance, frames, L, R, event count, events, transport, flag); the last two are stack arguments |
| `SetParameterA` | `0x2d60` | `0x20290` | (instance, index as int, value as float in `xmm0`) |
| `ResetA` | `0x2d80` | `0x20310` | (instance) |

The `CalcA` arguments that H4 and N5 left unknown, as the bundles read them:

- **Buffers.** `L` and `R` are processed in place: input in, output out.
- **Events.** 16 bytes each: a 64-bit frame offset, then status, data 1 and data 2 bytes. Corpus
  queues an event with frame − 1 when the frame is non-zero; Analog uses the frame as given.
- **Transport.** {int32 playing, padding, binary64 position, binary64 tempo}. Analog reads it
  unconditionally. Corpus accepts a null pointer.
- **Flag.** For Corpus, a non-zero `*flag` on entry means input is present; otherwise the engine
  reads silence. On return, Corpus clears it after fs/10 quiet frames (S). Analog writes 1 while
  it still sounds (X: 1 during the note, 0 after the release).
- **Block size.** `GetPlugInfoU` selector 5 answers 512 for both bundles. Corpus also processes
  internally in sub-blocks of at most 512 frames (S).

The selector answers measured (X) equal H4's static table.

**Not known (U).** Which frame base Live uses for events (0- or 1-based); what Live passes as the
transport and the input flag; Live's real block sizes; and when it runs the reset sequence.
The harness passes transport {0, 0.0, 120.0} and input flag 1 for effects (0 for instruments).

## Harness

[`harness/`](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/r2/t12-device-dsp-v1/harness/xdev.c) is a copy
of `tools/xexec` adapted to bundles. `tools/xexec` itself is unchanged. The copy is built to
`/var/tmp/sound-analysis/t12-xdev/xdev`. Changes:

- **Mapping.** The x86_64 slice is picked from the universal file and mapped at a fixed slide,
  `0x200000000`, because the image base 0 cannot be mapped. Rebases apply the slide.
  Zero-fill sections are cleared.
- **Imports.** Every unbound import gets its own slot in a `PROT_NONE` region. A call or a data
  read through it stops the run with `TRAP <symbol>`. The `__mod_init_func` initializers run
  after binding, as dyld would run them. Exports are found in the symbol table.
- **Driver.** A C script interpreter calls the exports directly, with real stack arguments for
  `CalcA`, and writes binary32 output. There is no limit of 256 calls.
- **Bindings.** Each binding keeps the contract its call sites rely on. `results/*.json` lists
  the imports each run used.

| Group | Binding |
| --- | --- |
| libc, memory, strings | glibc |
| libm | glibc 2.39. Bundles call `sinf`, `cosf`, `expf`, `logf`, `tanf`, `atanf`, `cbrt`, `sin`, `cos`, `exp`, `modff`, `ldexpf`, `floorf` and others; Tension and Electric also `__exp10`, `pow`, `log10f`, `fmodf` |
| macOS-only | `__sincosf_stret`, `__sincos_stret` (results packed as the ABI returns them), `memset_pattern16`, `newlocale` (C locale only; macOS mask values ignored), `__stderrp`, `_DefaultRuneLocale` (a C-locale table with Apple's `_CTYPE` bits, runetype at offset 60), `__maskrune` (no classes above 127) |
| `rand`, `srand` | The FreeBSD-lineage Park–Miller generator (16807, modulus 2³¹ − 1). This is inferred from Apple's libc lineage, not read from Apple's binary |
| `time` | Fixed at 0, or at `XDEV_TIME`. Both bundles seed `rand` with `srand(time(0))` while building their engines (Corpus `0x199c0`) |
| C++ ABI | `__dynamic_cast` and the three `__cxxabiv1` type_info vtables from libstdc++, which implements the same Itanium ABI. Exceptions, `__cxa_throw` and unwinding are trapped |
| libc++ (`std::__1`) | 59 members reimplemented in `xdev_cxx.c` on libc++'s x86_64 layouts: `std::string` standard layout (checked in the bundles' inline code), `ios_base`, `basic_ios`, `basic_streambuf` slots, `istream` (`get`, `unget`, `putback`, `tellg`, `seekg`, `>>` int and unsigned), `ostream` (`<<` int, unsigned long, bool, float, double; `put`, `flush`), the sentries, `locale` and `ctype<char>` |

The bundles need the stream shim. Corpus builds its module graph from text written to
`ostringstream`s and parsed with `sscanf`. It constructs its engine state from an embedded text
patch read through an `istringstream`. On every sample-rate change it saves that state through a
`stringstream` and reads it back. Floats are written with libc++'s default `%.*g`, precision 6,
and read back with `atof`. A traced rate change (`XDEV_TRACE_OSTREAM`) wrote 51 floats, all at
precision 6. No stream extraction set the fail bit (`cxx-failbits 0` in every log).

## Corpus results

The 64-render study below is the historical t12-device-dsp-v1 packet. The later
[finite Ramp capture review](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/full-spec-v1/reviews/corpus-lfo-ramp-up-review.json),
[math-sensitivity review](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/full-spec-v1/reviews/corpus-lfo-ramp-sensitivity-review.json)
and [finite engineering criterion review](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/full-spec-v1/reviews/corpus-lfo-ramp-criterion-review.json)
have separate bounded dispositions. They do not extend the older invariance
checks to new vectors, alter historical evidence, or establish Live/arm64
compatibility. Owner and handoff adoption remain separate review gates.

Cases: 64 renders of 16,384 frames at 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz ([`run_corpus.py`](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/r2/t12-device-dsp-v1/run_corpus.py)).
Each render goes CreateInstanceU, SetSampleRateU, then all 38 host values. Those are Live's
values for a fresh Corpus, equal to N5's `sent_for_default` (asserted), with the case's
changes. Then `CalcA` runs in 512-frame blocks. Inputs are unit impulses at frame 4,096, sines
of amplitude 0.25, seeded white noise (RMS 0.1) or a constant 0.5, the same on both channels.

### Determinism

| MXCSR flush-to-zero and denormals-are-zero (`0x9fc0`) | Bit-identical |

Interpretation. All three non-identical rate-history results come from the same step. The engine
is rebuilt on a rate change, and its state passes through six-significant-digit text. That is
deterministic, but it depends on history. ResetA alone turns a Tube into a Pipe (static quirk 4 of
the [resonator report](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/r2/corpus-dsp-v1/report-resonator.md)).
Live sends index 0 again after ResetA (S), and that undoes it. The random LFO shapes are seeded
from the clock each time the engine is built. Inferred: in Live their output differs between
instances and sessions, so no reference render can fix it.

Current interpretation (2026-10-19): the measured one-warm-up variant above is
preserved as executed harness evidence. It is not the four-helper grouped-reset
path later identified in Live, and never established Live insertion history.
No retained output, fixture history or released device comparison is changed.

### Static model

[`model.py`](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/r2/t12-device-dsp-v1/model.py) writes out the
static model of the modal types: controller curves, mode tables (from
`corpus-dsp-v1/tables.py`), damping, tilt, mode shapes, bleed, panpot, drive and width. It rounds
the per-mode coefficients to binary32. [`fit.py`](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/r2/t12-device-dsp-v1/fit.py)
measures each mode by demodulation: frequency, per-sample log radius and complex amplitude. It
measures the render and the model's own output with the same filter, and reports render minus
model. The "ulp bound" of a mode is the relative change that one binary32 ulp in each of its two
denominator coefficients would cause, plus 2⁻²³. The engine computes and stores these
coefficients in binary32, so the bound is the scale of an unavoidable difference. "Clean" modes
are those whose frequency the estimator recovers from the model's own output to 10⁻⁴. Results
are in `results/corpus_model.json`, summarised in `results/analyze_corpus.txt`.

| Quantity | Cases | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mode frequency | 41 impulse cases, all five modal types, 44.1 and 48 kHz, both channels | Every clean mode within 1.00 ulp bound (Membrane 1.19). Largest relative difference 3.3 × 10⁻⁴ at 66 Hz (bound 1.0 × 10⁻³); 2.6 × 10⁻⁵ or less above 250 Hz |
| Decay rate (log radius) | same | Within 2.04 ulp bounds of a2, except Membrane 4.4 and Plate 3.2, where closely spaced modes limit the estimator |
| Complex mode amplitude, render / model | same | Within 2.8 × 10⁻⁴ (Membrane 2.3 × 10⁻³) |
| Strike position, ExcitationX −1, −0.5, 0, 0.5, 1 | `hit_*` | Amplitudes follow the interpolated mode shapes, including host values below 0. At 0 the strike is at the centre, and harmonics 2, 4, 6 and 8 of the String are −119 to −122 dB below the fundamental (the model's own output: −119 to −121 dB, the estimator floor) |
| Pickups, ListeningXL and ListeningXR at 0, 0.5, 1 | `listen*` | Left follows XL and right follows XR. The other channel is unchanged within 4.1 × 10⁻²¹ |
| Input routing, impulse on the left input only | `left_only` | Right output at most 2 × 10⁻²⁰; left output bit-identical to `defaults_44` |
| Inharmonics ±0.5, AmpFreq ±1, Ratio 0.2/0.8 (Membrane), Bleed 1 | 7 of the 41 cases | Frequencies within 0.67 ulp bounds; amplitudes within 1.1 × 10⁻⁴ |
| Waveform, first 256 samples after the impulse | the 41 cases | Rel RMS 4 × 10⁻⁶ to 8 × 10⁻⁵; 6 × 10⁻⁴ at 66 Hz |
| Pitch mapping | `pitch_*`, `detune_0.5` | Frequency 0.5625 gives 377.341 Hz. The model's 2ˣ approximation gives 377.339 Hz and exact 2ᵖ gives 377.122 Hz (+1.0 cent). Detune 0.5 gives 269.346 Hz left and 254.176 Hz right |
| Steady-state sine gain | 8 cases (261.62 to 3,000 Hz; DryWet 0.25, 0.5; Drive 0, 1; 48 kHz) | Within 2.2 × 10⁻⁴ dB and 0.057° (at 261.62 Hz, on resonance) |
| Noise | `noise_44` | Rel RMS 1.6 × 10⁻⁴ from frame 8,192; third-octave levels within 0.0039 dB |
| LFO, Sine, rate 0.8, amount 0.3 | `lfo_sine` | Rate 2.539 Hz, ±0.302 octave, right channel half a cycle later. Instantaneous pitch within 0.095 Hz of the model when the pitch is read every 200 frames. Without that update rule the error is 2.09 Hz |
| Start-up, DryWet 0, constant input | `startup_dry` | Frames 0–99 are silent. The gain rises linearly from 0.009743 at frame 100 to 0.974309 at frame 199 and stays there. P(0.25) = 0.9743092 |

The waveform comparison over 8,192 samples is 1 × 10⁻⁴ to 2 × 10⁻³ (10⁻² at 66 Hz). That is
phase drift from the sub-ulp frequency differences, not a shape difference.

### Port

[`port_render.js`](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/r2/t12-device-dsp-v1/port_render.js)
runs the engine embedded in the demo page on every case (`results/corpus_port.json`).

| Cases | Port against X |
| --- | --- |
| Modal impulse cases | Rel RMS 1 × 10⁻⁴ to 5 × 10⁻³; 4 × 10⁻² at 66 Hz |
| Sine, noise, filter, drive, DryWet from frame 8,192 | Rel RMS 1.7 × 10⁻⁷ to 5.2 × 10⁻⁴; noise and filters within 0.0039 dB per third octave |
| The same from frame 0 | 4 % to 11 % |
| Pipe and Tube, eight cases, including Radius 0 and 1 and Opening 0.3 | Rel RMS 3.8 × 10⁻⁶ to 1.9 × 10⁻⁵ from the impulse on; fundamentals equal within 3 × 10⁻⁷ (2 × 10⁻⁸ at the default Radius and Opening) |
| LFO sine | The same pitch trajectory within 0.035 Hz; time-domain 0.1 from phase drift |
| LFO Sample & Hold | Not comparable: the port uses `Math.random` |

Fundamentals (X): Pipe 251.096 Hz at 44.1 kHz and 251.271 Hz at 48 kHz; Tube 241.364 and
241.687 Hz; Pipe with Radius 1 258.339 Hz; Pipe with Opening 0.3 234.444 Hz; Tube with Radius 0
173.067 Hz. All are at Frequency 0.5, nominally 261.62 Hz.

### Differences and their explanations

1. **Start-up.** The port ramps the panpot gains from frame 0. The original is silent for the
   first 100 frames and then ramps over the next 100. This explains the port's 4–11 % error on
   inputs that start at frame 0. The report's "both gains start at 0" is right, but it leaves out
   that the first ramp starts only after 100 frames.
2. **Precision.** The port computes the coefficients in binary64. The original computes and
   stores them in binary32. Low modes are the most sensitive, and their phase drifts: rel RMS
   4 × 10⁻² at 66 Hz. The static model with binary32 rounding is within one ulp bound.
3. **Pipe and Tube pitch.** [Corpus DSP](corpus-dsp-static.md#quirks-a-faithful-port-reproduces)
   quirk 4 and CD-C07 give 251.0 Hz and 241.2 Hz at 48 kHz. X and the port both give 251.271
   and 241.687 Hz; the page's figures were rough readings. The drops are 3.96 % and 7.62 %, as
   CD-C07 says ("about 4 % and 8 %").
4. **Sample-rate change.** Not in the static model: the six-digit state round trip
   ([determinism](#determinism)).
5. **DecayOnNoteOff.** The port passes the stored value through the D rule. Live sends 1 − v
   (N5). It matters only while MidiGate closes the gate, which no case exercised.
6. **Random LFO shapes.** These are seeded from the clock in the original and from `Math.random`
   in the port. Neither can be compared sample by sample.

No difference between X and the static model remains unexplained.

## Analog results

`results/analog.json`, renders in `raw/analog/`. The "live" parameter set sends all 203 indices
with N6's `sent_for_default`. Second outputs follow N6's rules: sign flags 0, the
filter-type low bit 0, the Polyphony flag 1. Mode-2 entries whose toggles default off send 0.
The "bundle" set sends nothing.

- **Notes in, audio out.** C4 (note 60, velocity 100) at frame 4,096, released 22,050 frames
  later:

  | Parameters, rate | Peak | Fundamental | Last frame above 10⁻⁶ |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Live defaults, 44.1 kHz | 0.324 | 261.537 Hz | 53,669 |
  | Live defaults, 48 kHz | 0.323 | 261.478 Hz | 56,055 |
  | The engine's built-in patch | 0.100 | — | 26,180 |

  A4 gives 440.193 Hz. Both channels are identical. The activity flag is 1 for
  the first two host blocks after creation, then 0 until a note and 1 while sounding
  ([Analog protocol](device-analog-dsp.md#corrections-to-other-pages)). C4’s
  261.537 Hz is the rounded result of the settled pitch law, not an error
  relative to 12-TET; see [Analog DSP](device-analog-dsp.md).
- **Determinism.** A re-run, blocks of 64 and 256, and flush-to-zero with denormals-are-zero are
  all bit-identical. Blocks of 333 differ, because the note-on falls inside a block and is
  placed differently (below).
- **Event timing (X, exact).** Analog keeps a control grid of ⌊fs/1000⌋ frames (44 at 44.1 kHz,
  48 at 48 kHz), counted from the first processed frame. An event at frame f takes effect at the
  start g of the grid period containing f. If g lies in an earlier host block, it takes effect at
  g plus one period instead. The first changed output frame is the one after that point. A
  note-on can therefore sound up to a period minus one frame before its timestamp. The rule held
  for all 250 note-ons (blocks of 512, 64 and 333; both rates) and all 50 note-offs tested
  (`timing`).
- **Follow-up 2026-10-19.** [Analog DSP](device-analog-dsp.md) and
  [the Analog draft](../specs/device-analog.md) now specify parameter meaning,
  polyphony and voice allocation with exact pitch/filter/envelope laws. Their
  remaining gaps are canonical there; the feasibility run above is historical.

**Current disposition (2026-10-20):** that historical inference is not an
adopted error bound. Finite perturbation observations do not establish the
actual Apple-library error, a general transfer bound, or a graded expectation
for omitted/underfit modes. Use the canonical draft's explicit per-case
criteria and unknowns; retain this paragraph as the original study's inference.


**arm64.** [`fma_scan.py`](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/r2/t12-device-dsp-v1/fma_scan.py)
decodes the arm64 `__text` (`results/fma_scan.txt`):

- Corpus has 232 fused multiply-adds in 54 functions: 67 in the modal resonator `0x1f3f8`, 36 in
  the waveguide `0x23670`, 7 in the panpot `0x1dfec` and 6 in the parameter wrapper `0xd75c`.
- Analog has 814 in 130 functions.
- The x86_64 slices have none.

Inferred: arm64 renders differ from x86_64 renders by rounding at the same scale as the libm
effect. That is about one ulp bound in each mode frequency, plus per-sample rounding in the
filters. It was not measured: no arm64 execution is authorised.

**Consequence for the metric.** A maximum absolute error over a long resonant render is not
usable. A one-ulp change moves a 66 Hz mode by 6.8 × 10⁻⁴, which is 4 % of the waveform after
0.28 s. Per-device metrics must therefore be stated as follows.

- **Corpus modal types**, on the modes the estimator resolves in the reference render:
  - per-mode frequency within 4 ulp bounds;
  - decay rate within 8 ulp bounds of a2;
  - complex amplitude within 5 × 10⁻³.

  Elsewhere the largest render-to-model differences are 1.0 ulp bound, 2.04 ulp bounds and
  2.8 × 10⁻⁴, a margin of four or more. On Membrane they are 1.19, 4.4 and 2.3 × 10⁻³, a margin
  of only about two, because its closely spaced modes limit the estimator. For all Corpus modal
  types:
  - waveform rel RMS within 2 × 10⁻³ over the first 256 samples after an impulse;
  - third-octave noise levels within 0.01 dB;
  - sine steady-state within 0.01 dB and 0.5°.
- **Pipe, Tube and the non-resonant paths.** These are insensitive (10⁻⁵), so a waveform rel RMS
  of 10⁻⁴ over the whole render can be used.
- **Analog.** Event timing exact, and f0 within 10⁻⁵. Its spectral metric is to be calibrated in
  its own spec.

[SPEC-DEVICE-CORPUS](../specs/device-corpus.md#comparison-metric) states the Corpus metric.

## Limits

- **What X shows.** X shows what the x86_64 bundle code computes for the stated calls, inputs and
  substitutions. It does not show that Live makes the same calls. The call sequence is N5's
  static reading, with the unknowns listed [above](#how-live-drives-a-bundle).
- **Substitutions.** These are libm, `rand` (inferred Park–Miller), `time`, the C locale and the
  libc++ shim. The shim follows libc++ semantics for well-formed text. Overflow and other error
  paths of stream extraction were never reached, and they are implemented only to libc++'s
  documented behavior.
- **The arm64 slice was not executed.** Its fused operations are counted only.
- **Coverage.** Corpus's MIDI side-chain (MidiPitch, MidiGate, Transpose, FineTranspose,
  PitchBendRange, DecayOnNoteOff, MidiMode), LFO Sync and Spin, filter settings other than the two
  rendered, and parameter changes during a render were not run.
- **Retention.** The renders in `raw/` are retained. Re-runs, variants, sweeps, perturbed and port
  renders are written to `/var/tmp/sound-analysis/t12-xdev/`. Their SHA-256 and comparisons are
  in `results/`.
