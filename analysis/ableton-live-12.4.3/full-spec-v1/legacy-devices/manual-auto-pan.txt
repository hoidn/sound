auto-pan:B001 HTML:318 24.3 Auto Pan
auto-pan:B002 HTML:330 Auto Pan offers LFO-driven manipulation of amplitude and panning for creating automatic panning, tremolo and amplitude modulation, and beat-synchronized chopping effects.
auto-pan:B003 HTML:330 Auto Pan’s LFOs modulate the amplitude of the left and right stereo channels with sine, triangle, sawtooth down or random waveforms.
auto-pan:B004 HTML:330 The Shape control pushes the waveform to its upper and lower limits, ”hardening” its shape. The waveform can be set to ”Normal” or ”Invert” (use ”Invert” to, for example, create the saw up waveform from the saw down waveform).
auto-pan:B005 HTML:330 LFO speed is controlled with the Rate control, which can be set in terms of hertz. Rate can also be synced to the song tempo.
auto-pan:B006 HTML:330 Though both LFOs run at the same frequency, the Phase control lends the sound stereo movement by offsetting their waveforms relative to each other. Set this to ”180”, and the LFOs will be perfectly out of phase (180 degrees apart), so that when one reaches its peak, the other is at its minimum. Phase is particularly effective for creating vibrato effects.
auto-pan:B007 HTML:330 The Offset control shifts the start point of each LFO along its waveform.
auto-pan:B008 HTML:330 The device’s influence on incoming signals is set with the Amount control.
