# Delay/reverb device documentary and installed-file evidence

## Scope and canonical owner

The [individual device draft](../specs/delay-reverb-devices.md) owns Delay, Echo,
Filter Delay, Grain Delay, Reverb, Hybrid Reverb and Resonators behavior under
FEAT-DEVICES. This page owns retained evidence interpretation, not competing
normative clauses. The [inventory](full-live-coverage-inventory.md),
[coverage design](full-live-specification-design.md), [execution plan](full-live-specification-plan.md)
and [project schema](../schema.md) govern the partial packet. Device-rack,
mix/automation, tuning and media owners are linked from the draft; none supplies
these devices' missing DSP.

## Documentary distinctions and limits

Observed in source, not execution: Delay Freeze ignores new input while looping
buffered content (delay:B008); Reverb Freeze sustains diffuse response with
Flat/Cut modifiers (reverb:B022); Hybrid Reverb Freeze/Freeze In apply to its
algorithm engine (hybrid-reverb:B031). These are distinct user-facing admission
contracts, not evidence of shared internal storage or a common kernel.

Observed in source: Echo has internal Gate/Ducking and envelope/LFO mixing
(echo:B016–B026), not a documented external-sidechain interface in this bounded
chapter. Its shutdown description (echo:B030) says at least eight seconds after
silent input and a Noise+Gate exception. That cannot become an exact eight-second
tail cutoff, an external CPU-use metric or a mandated candidate implementation.
DR-REVIEW-001 identifies that the statement supplies no application-observable
shutdown outcome. REQ-DR-ECHO-014 and DR-ECHO-014 therefore remain U with a null
probe expectation, preserving the source locator and four-state probe action.

Observed in source: Hybrid Serial's algorithm receives convolution output
(hybrid-reverb:B014); its Blend 0/100 endpoint differs from raw-input Algorithm
routing. Single-engine Blend is described as ineffective (B016–B019). User IR
folder addition and remove/reinsert list reset (B024) are distinct from saved
media reference persistence. Bass Mono's below-180-Hz description (B082) does not
settle dry participation, crossover filters or equality at 180 Hz.

Observed in source: Resonators distinguishes scale-relative and tuning-index
contexts (resonators:B008–B011), channel assignments (B007), and the wet II–V
Width scope (B012). No note-frequency, pan sum or decay formula follows.

Inference: matching installed fields and current manual terminology provide a
useful profile crosswalk. They do **not** resolve exact-build version parity,
constructor defaults or DSP. No independent original/candidate or extracted-code
measurement was retained. In particular random waveform labels, Spray/Random
Pitch, Noise/Wobble and Prism descriptions supply no seed/distribution/statistic;
nominal time labels supply no rounding/smoothing law; -60 dB Decay definitions
supply no calibrated waveform tolerance.

## Cases, nonvacuity and unresolved behavior

The draft contains **92 requirements: 77 D12 and 15 U**. Its
[case packet](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/full-spec-v1/delay-reverb/cases.json)
contains **149 authored finite definitions: 77 D12, seven Dpkg and 65 U**.
All exact-target expected values are null; U documentary/runtime expectations
are also null. Documentary predicate checks and package-parser checks cannot be
counted as application compatibility passes. The Dpkg cases validate retained
preset identity/field presence only and do not satisfy the U lifecycle requirement
to which their evidence trail is attached.

The [fixtures](../../analysis/ableton-live-12.4.3/full-spec-v1/delay-reverb/fixtures.json)
require an active signal path, nonzero relevant stimulus and wet output,
baseline plus positive control, and isolation of other contributing paths.
Delay/Reverb/Hybrid admission tests use distinct A/B signals; Hybrid uses public
routing/Blend controls to isolate algorithmic output instead of requiring hidden
intermediate-buffer access. Filter/modulation tests excite the relevant bands
with enabled destination/depth. Channel-input exclusions test a matching-channel
response first. A failed gate stays inconclusive.

Per device, U cases retain automation, persistence, undo/redo, malformed/corrupt
input, cancellation, teardown, silence/tail, sample-rate/block and external-sidechain
questions. Further open interactions include tempo/transport transitions,
real-time/offline scheduling, fractional delays, interrupted IR load, engine
restart, missing IRs, frozen-tail storage, sample-rate changes during a tail,
latency compensation, and negative/extreme feedback. Their exact errors,
cleanup, numeric laws and tolerances remain unknown. Existing generic host,
rack, media and automation contracts cannot close these device-specific gaps.
