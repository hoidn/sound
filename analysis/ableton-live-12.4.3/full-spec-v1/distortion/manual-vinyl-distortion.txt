vinyl-distortion:B001 HTML:2228 29.41 Vinyl Distortion
vinyl-distortion:B002 HTML:2240 The Vinyl Distortion effect emulates some of the typical distortions that occur on vinyl records during playback. These distortions are caused by the geometric relationships between the needle and the recorded groove. The effect also features a crackle generator for adding noisy artifacts.
vinyl-distortion:B003 HTML:2240 The Tracing Model section adds even harmonic distortion to the input signal. Adjust the amount of distortion with the Drive knob, or click and drag vertically in the Tracing Model X-Y display. To adjust the distortion’s frequency or ”color,” drag horizontally in the X-Y display or double-click on the Freq field and type in a value. Holding the Alt (Win) / Option (Mac) modifier while dragging vertically in the X-Y display changes the frequency band’s Q (bandwidth).
vinyl-distortion:B004 HTML:2240 The Pinch Effect section adds odd harmonics to the input signal. These distortions typically occur 180 degrees out of phase, creating a richer stereo image. The Pinch Effect has the same controls as the Tracing Model, but generates a rather different sound.
vinyl-distortion:B005 HTML:2240 The Drive control increases or decreases the overall distortion amount created by both the Tracing Model and Pinch.
vinyl-distortion:B006 HTML:2240 There are two distortion modes: Soft and Hard. The Soft Mode simulates the sound of a dub plate, while Hard Mode is more like that of a standard vinyl record.
vinyl-distortion:B007 HTML:2240 The stereo/mono switch determines whether the Pinch distortion occurs in stereo or mono. Set it to stereo for realistic simulation of vinyl distortions.
vinyl-distortion:B008 HTML:2240 The Crackle section adds noise to the signal, with noise density set by the Density control. The Volume control adjusts the amount of gain applied to the noise.
