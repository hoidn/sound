5. Q-019 status (proposed)
--------------------------
  Settled statically (S), with conditions. Stored volume gain g is sent as position p = min(1, max(0,
  SControlLevel(1, g))) (manual: AUserFloat slot 0x208; create: ATimeableFloat slot 0x310; envelope events:
  owner provider, see 1d); the engine applies SAudioLevel(1, p), the crossfade factor where wired, and the
  floor 0.00032 -> 0. Round trip p -> g is exact in range (X2, per-object conversions 0x101aecbd0/0x101aecc90;
  the by-mode registry variants 0x101aed3a0/0x101aed490 are the same code over the global registry, S).
  Out of range: g > 1.99526238 -> p = 1 -> +6 dB (1.99526238); g < 0.0003162277571 (incl. 0, negative) -> p = 0 ->
  0.0003162277571 -> floored to silence. Sends: same with kind 0 (send curve), AConvertLevel(0) and the floor.
  Remaining conditions: (i) owner of a loaded envelope = AFloatAutomationTarget (1d); (ii) AConvertLevel(0)
  node connected to Send<k> (3c); (iii) mocker flag unset (2d); (iv) arm64 equivalence of 1d and 3b-3c not compared.
